## How to fill this in

For each cell, write in your own words, from your own programme experience:

- **Behaviour** — what a venture at this stage has observably done. Things a manager could check, not attitudes or intentions.
- **Proof** — what a founder could show to prove it (for example: interview log, signed letter of intent, invoice, test report, filing receipt, cap table, bank statement extract).
- **Strong vs weak** — which of those proofs you would accept on its own, and which only count alongside something else.

Rules:

- Do not copy or adapt wording from any published framework, including the level descriptors in the legacy app. Write from what you have seen ventures do.
- Rough notes are fine. One or two lines per cell is enough; the 20-word descriptors are drafted from these.
- If two neighbouring stages feel the same for a dimension, say so rather than forcing a difference.
